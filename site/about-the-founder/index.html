Justin earned his bachelor’s degree in athletic training at Brigham Young University in 2019 and has held his Certified Athletic Trainer credential since then. His experience spans competitive and professional athletes, dancers, endurance and strength athletes, and active adults working toward everyday goals.

Before building Athlecare, he worked with Recharge Sports Performance and Carolina Crown. Across athletic and performing-arts settings, he learned to adapt support to the demands of the person: their activity, their recovery, their circumstances, and the next step they could realistically take.

A practical care background

Movement assessment, stability, progressive training, recovery, and injury-prevention education inform his work. He respects professional scope and the need to collaborate with or refer to qualified clinicians when appropriate.

A lasting-capacity perspective

Temporary relief is one part of the picture. Justin’s approach also considers the control, strength, routines, and support that help people continue participating in the activities they value.

The belief behind Kivi

Everyone deserves the opportunity to grow.
No one should have to find their way alone.

This is the foundation of Kivi’s purpose. People need more than information: they need practical tools, meaningful feedback, and relationships that help them move forward. The leaders providing that support need an environment that supports their work, too.

2019

Professional foundation

BYU athletic training education and Certified Athletic Trainer credential established the foundation for individualized recovery and performance work.

2021

Athlecare begins

Justin founded Athlecare to make meaningful injury recovery and performance support more accessible. Early work included hands-on care and conversations at local trailheads.

Today

Care, delivery, and business operations

Athlecare grew to serve people through locations in Pleasant Grove and Draper, Utah, alongside virtual coaching. That work brings care delivery, client relationships, and running a service business into the same real-world context.

Kivi

Tools for the people helping others grow

Kivi connects software, organizational programs, and franchise opportunities. Athlecare is its first software client, using Kivi within corporate wellness and sports team support programs.
