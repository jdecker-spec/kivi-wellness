6. Artificial Intelligence and Automated Processing

Kivi may use artificial intelligence to support general wellness education and recommendations relating to training, nutrition, recovery, and personal capacity.

AI features may analyze information supplied through check-ins, capacity practices, training logs, nutrition records, recovery records, and other permitted data sources.

Kivi’s capacity framework may organize personal wellness information into Mind, Body, Emotion, and Spirit domains.

The Mind-domain score uses information supplied through check-ins and capacity practices to estimate nonclinical wellness capacity and suggest general capacity-building activities.

Kivi’s AI is not intended to operate as a mental-health chatbot or provide mental-health diagnosis, psychotherapy, or treatment.

Mental-health concerns requiring professional evaluation or treatment are outside the AI’s intended scope and may prompt recommendations to consult an appropriately qualified professional.

Kivi AI does not independently prescribe medication, issue medical diagnoses, or change instructions provided by licensed healthcare professionals.

AI outputs and capacity scores may be incomplete or inaccurate and are not substitutes for individualized professional medical advice.

When third-party AI processors are used, personal information may be transmitted to those processors only for appropriately disclosed, permitted purposes and subject to applicable privacy requirements.

Additional information about sensitive health-data processing appears in our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy.

Kivi may provide wellness-related features through employers, sports teams, health professionals, or other organizations.

Such organizations may have access to administrative details necessary to manage their contracted Services.

Where authorized, professional users may access relevant information through their assigned account permissions.

Organizational reporting may use aggregated or de-identified information with appropriate safeguards against reidentification, including for small participant groups.

Additional obligations may apply under employment, disability, medical privacy, and nondiscrimination laws.

Kivi’s website may use cookies and similar technologies to support:

Login sessions and account security.

Website preferences.

Form submissions and spam prevention.

Website performance measurement.

Analytics and service improvements.

Advertising functions where implemented.

Users can manage cookies through browser settings and any consent controls made available on the Site.

10. Advertising and Marketing

Some free or discounted Kivi services may be supported by advertising or other promotional arrangements.

Advertising and analytics providers may process limited device, advertising, or usage information, depending on the applicable product, user choices, and permissions.

We do not authorize identifiable consumer health data to be used for targeted advertising without satisfying all applicable legal requirements.

Where required, users will have the ability to grant or withhold consent for tracking and advertising-related processing.

Kivi does not sell personal information as a business practice described by this policy. If any processing is legally classified as a sale or regulated sharing, Kivi will provide required disclosures and consumer choices before engaging in that processing.

Marketing communications can be opted out of using the applicable unsubscribe mechanism.

11. Payments and Subscriptions

Kivi may offer free and paid subscriptions for individual users, coaches, trainers, and organizations.

Payment transactions may be processed through third-party payment providers or app stores.

We may retain transaction records reasonably necessary for accounting, customer service, fraud prevention, billing disputes, and legal compliance.

Subscription cancellation controls are provided through supported account settings or the applicable app-store subscription interface.

Account deletion may not automatically cancel an externally billed subscription.

12. Email, SMS, and Other Communications

Kivi may communicate with users about account activity, subscriptions, security, support, appointments, and service-related matters.

Users who affirmatively opt in to SMS communications may receive messages related to the specific program for which they enrolled.

Message frequency may vary. Message and data rates may apply.

Where supported, reply STOP to unsubscribe from text messages or HELP for assistance.

Marketing SMS messages require any additional consent required by applicable law.

Kivi does not sell or share mobile messaging opt-in information with unrelated third parties for their marketing purposes.

Messaging service providers may process phone numbers and related delivery information as needed to operate authorized communications.

Users may unsubscribe from promotional emails through the unsubscribe link provided.

13. Data Security

Kivi uses administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, and improper disclosure.

Safeguards may include access restrictions, authentication controls, monitoring, and protections provided through selected infrastructure providers.

The specific safeguards applied depend on the systems, data sensitivity, and associated risks.

No electronic system can be guaranteed completely secure.

We will respond to security incidents and provide legally required notifications, including under applicable health-data breach notification requirements.

14. Data Retention

Kivi retains personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide requested Services, maintain accounts, fulfill disclosed purposes, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Retention periods may depend on:

The type and sensitivity of information.

The user’s account and preferences.

Whether the information is still needed for a requested service.

Legal, financial, or security requirements.

Applicable privacy and deletion laws.

Necessary backup and recovery processes.

Users may request deletion through the supported account-deletion process or contact Kivi directly.

Information may remain where lawful retention exceptions apply. Deletion from backup systems will follow applicable legal requirements and technical retention procedures.

16. Account Cancellation and Deletion

Kivi provides account management features that allow users to manage applicable consents, subscriptions, and account deletion.

Users may request deletion through their account settings.

Users who cannot access the App, including those who have uninstalled it, may also initiate an account deletion request by emailing:

Request Account Deletion

Please include the email address associated with the account. Do not include passwords, payment card numbers, or sensitive medical records in the request.

Kivi may verify account ownership before completing the request.

Deletion includes associated personal data as required by law, subject to applicable lawful retention exceptions.

A user may need to cancel an Apple or Google subscription separately to prevent future app-store charges.

17. Children’s Privacy

Kivi’s standard individual account service is intended for adults aged 18 or older unless a separately authorized youth program is made available.

We do not knowingly direct the standard service to children under 13.

If we learn that information was collected from a child in a manner inconsistent with applicable law, we will take appropriate action.

A parent or guardian may contact Kivi with concerns about a minor’s information.
