Privacy Policy

How Kivi handles website inquiries, booking information, and privacy requests.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Who operates this website?

Kivi Wellness is operated by Athlecare Franchising LLC, 564 West 700 South, Suite 204, Pleasant Grove, UT 84062, United States. Privacy requests: kivifranchise@gmail.com.

What information does the website collect?

The inquiry form collects your name, email address, optional organization, and area of interest. We use these details to respond to your inquiry and discuss the Kivi services you request. The form does not request health or medical information. Please do not include medical records, symptoms, passwords, or payment details.

When you visit, our hosting and infrastructure providers process technical information needed to deliver and protect the website, such as your IP address, browser information, requested pages, and security logs.

Who processes inquiry and booking information?

  • Cloudflare hosts and delivers this website. Website source is maintained through GitHub.
  • FormSubmit processes form entries and sends them to our business email at justin@kiviwellness.com. Its spam check uses Google reCAPTCHA, which may process technical browser and interaction information.
  • Microsoft 365 / Outlook and GoDaddy email services support our business email and email security.
  • Calendly processes scheduling information when you choose to book a discovery call. Booking takes place on Calendly's website.

These providers process information according to their roles and their own applicable notices: Cloudflare, FormSubmit, Google, Microsoft, GoDaddy, and Calendly.

Cookies, analytics, and external content

The redesigned website does not currently load Google Analytics or advertising pixels. Essential security and form technologies may use cookies or similar mechanisms on their own pages. We will update this notice and implement applicable choices before adding optional tracking.

Some photographs load from Unsplash, which receives technical information when your browser requests an image. External websites, app stores, telemedicine providers, and other linked services have their own privacy practices.

How do we use and disclose information?

We use business inquiry details to respond, arrange requested conversations, evaluate service fit, maintain records of our communications, protect our systems, and meet applicable legal obligations. Submitting an inquiry is not a newsletter signup. We do not sell website inquiry information or use it for targeted advertising. We disclose information to the service providers needed for these purposes and when legally required.

Retention and security

We retain inquiry correspondence for as long as reasonably needed to respond, manage the business relationship, meet legal obligations, and resolve disputes. You can request deletion. Relevant service providers may retain their own operational records under their policies. No website or email system can guarantee complete security.

Your privacy choices and requests

To request access, correction, deletion, or other rights available under applicable law, email kivifranchise@gmail.com or write to our address above. We may verify your identity or authority before fulfilling a request. We will respond within the time required by applicable law. If a request is denied, you may ask for reconsideration by emailing the same address with the subject "Privacy appeal." Please do not send sensitive medical information to verify your identity.

Consumer health data and separate services

This notice describes the marketing website and its business inquiry and scheduling flow. The website does not create an app account or collect app check-ins, wearable information, training logs, nutrition logs, or capacity scores. App subscriptions, employer or team programs, and clinical services involve separate systems and service-specific notices and agreements. Review the notice presented by the service before providing personal or health information.

Read our separate Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy for the marketing website. Telemedicine is delivered through a separate provider with its own privacy notice.

Children and changes to this notice

The business inquiry website is intended for adults and does not solicit information from children under 13. Contact us if a child has submitted information. Updates to this notice will be posted here with a revised date.
